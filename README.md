# Mississippi Real Estate Course

Self-study salesperson pre-licensing prep for Mississippi, as a single-file web app (`index.html`).

- 18 units, 43 lessons, 229 practice questions
- Part 1: national principles. Part 2: Mississippi license law, brokerage relationships, trust accounts, homestead and foreclosure, landlord-tenant, property tax, Gulf Coast flood/wind/tidelands
- 19 types of random math drills with worked solutions
- 120-question exam simulator (80 national at 70%, 40 Mississippi at 75%)
- Progress saves in the browser only (localStorage)

## Use
Open `index.html` in any browser, or host the repo with GitHub Pages (Settings > Pages > deploy from `main`, root).

## Edit
Course content lives in `source/content/*.py`. The app shell is `source/template.html`.
Rebuild with `python3 source/build.py` (writes `index.html`).

## Disclaimer
Study aid only. Not legal advice and not an official MREC or PSI publication. Built from the MREC license law (rev. July 1, 2026), MREC forms, and standard national principles. Several items are flagged "Confirm before you rely on it" inside the lessons. Verify current law, forms, fees and exam rules with the Mississippi Real Estate Commission (mrec.ms.gov) and the current PSI candidate bulletin.
